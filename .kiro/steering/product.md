# Product: Séance, the cross-market odds medium
- Séance finds the same real-world event priced on multiple venues (Polymarket, Kalshi, crypto spot/DEX), fuses the prices into one consensus probability, and alerts when venues diverge.
- Users: prediction-market traders, quant researchers, and AI agents (through MCP).
- Theme: a Halloween Ouija board. The theme must drive function: planchette = consensus, "possession" = divergence, "spirits" = venues. It's never decoration only.
- Non-goals: placing trades, holding user funds, financial advice. Always show a "for research only" disclaimer.
- Kiroween category: Frankenstein (stitching incompatible tech into one app).
